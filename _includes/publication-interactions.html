{%- comment -%} Progressive enhancement for the publication lists. * The author list is rendered in full by _plugins/jekyll_scholar.rb, with the middle authors inside `.au-rest[hidden]`. Clicking the ellipsis reveals them; nothing is fetched, so it works offline and on a static host. * The abstract is emitted by _layouts/bibtemplate.html when the .bib entry has one, and starts hidden. With JavaScript disabled the page still shows the truncated author list and simply omits the toggles, which is the behaviour the site had before. {%- endcomment -%}
