{%- comment -%} Grants renderer. Kept separate from cv-section.html because grants are the one dataset whose shape genuinely differs: the title is quoted with language-specific quote marks, and an optional amount / grant number is appended in parentheses. Folding that into the generic include would need a quoting-mode parameter and two conditional tails -- more total complexity than a short sibling. `grant_number` and `amount` are simply absent from the generated YAML when the corresponding show_*_web flag is FALSE, so the presence test below is the whole of the display-flag logic on this side. Params: rows (array, required), lang ("en" | "ja") {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign lang = include.lang | default: "en" -%}
    {%- for row in include.rows -%} {%- assign d = row.date[lang] | default: row.date.en -%} {%- assign agency = row.agency[lang] | default: row.agency.en -%} {%- assign program = row.program[lang] | default: row.program.en -%} {%- assign title = row.title[lang] | default: row.title.en -%} {%- assign amount = row.amount[lang] | default: row.amount.en -%} {%- assign extra = "" -%} {%- if row.grant_number and row.grant_number != "" -%}{%- assign extra = row.grant_number -%}{%- endif -%} {%- if amount and amount != "" -%} {%- if extra == "" -%}{%- assign extra = amount -%} {%- else -%}{%- assign extra = extra | append: ", " | append: amount -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endif -%}
